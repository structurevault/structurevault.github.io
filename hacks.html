💚 Hacking for good: white hats

Not every hacker is a villain. White-hat hackers use the same skills to protect people, find bugs before criminals do (with permission), and build new tools that make life fairer, like money anyone can use with just a phone.

🤍 White hatHas permission. Finds problems and helps fix them.
🩶 Grey hatNo permission, but means well. Still against the rules.
🖤 Black hatBreaks in to steal, lock up or damage things.

Videos are for classroom discussion. Never try hacking anything you don't own or have permission to test.
