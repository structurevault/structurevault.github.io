🌱 How a wallet is born: 12 secret words

⚠ DEMO ONLY: never use words made on a website for real bitcoin

1. The wallet flips 128 coins

A new wallet starts with randomness: 128 coin flips. Each flip is a bit, either 0 or 1.

128 flips can come out 340 undecillion different ways (a 3 with 38 zeros after it). That’s more than the number of grains of sand on every beach on Earth, so nobody can guess yours.

2. Add a check, then chop into 12 pieces

The wallet scrambles the 128 bits with a math recipe called SHA-256 and sticks the first 4 bits of the answer on the end. This checksum catches typos later. Now there are 132 bits, chopped into 12 pieces of 11 bits. Each piece is a number from 0 to 2047.

3. Each number picks a word

Every wallet in the world uses the same official list of 2048 words (called BIP39), from abandon to zoo. Each number picks one word: number 0 is abandon, number 2047 is zoo. Words are much easier to write down than 0s and 1s!

These 12 words are the wallet. The last word includes the checksum, so if you write one word wrong, the wallet will notice.

4. The words grow into keys and addresses

The wallet mixes the words 2048 times to make a big secret seed. From the seed it makes private keys, and from each private key a public address to receive bitcoin.

(The seed above is real math for these words; the addresses are pretend.)

5. Keep your 12 words secret and safe

Whoever has the 12 words owns the bitcoin. There’s no “forgot password” button and no bank to call.

✅ Write them on paper, in order
✅ Keep the paper somewhere safe, like a family safe
✅ Your wallet app makes the words for you, offline
❌ Never take a photo or screenshot
❌ Never type them into a website or message
❌ Never tell anyone, even “helpers”. It’s a scam!
