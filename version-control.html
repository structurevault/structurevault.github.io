HOW IT WORKS

🧱

COMMIT

Every change a builder makes is a block on the timeline. Small changes, made often, with your name on them. No mystery edits.

🔍

TEAM REVIEW

At the end of each sprint the team runs its own meeting and checks the build against the defence plan. Everyone votes.

✅

VERSION BUMP

Unanimous tick? The build is tagged as the next version: v1.1, v1.2, v1.3... That tag is a save point you can always get back to.

💥

ROLLBACK

No consensus? Roll back to the last tagged version. The failed version number isn't skipped, it just has to wait: the team earns it in the next sprint instead.
